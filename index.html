README / About This Job Board

Purpose

This resource provides a curated list of U.S.-based academic and research employment opportunities aligned with VCU’s Research, Assessment, and Evaluation (RAE) program, Advanced Educational Statistics Certificate, and CENTROID Applied Machine Learning Lab.

Search Process

The search task is automated with ChatGPT and uses a multi-pass search across academic job boards, disciplinary sources, and official employer career pages. Results are screened for methodological relevance, duplicates, and posting status. Automated results should not be interpreted as exhaustive.

Search Areas

Quantitative methods, educational statistics, multilevel modeling, SEM, longitudinal and latent variable methods, educational measurement and psychometrics, evaluation and assessment, qualitative and mixed methods, critical and equity-oriented methods, machine learning, AI in education, educational data science, learning analytics, and research methodology.

Position Types

Assistant, Associate, Full, and Open Rank faculty; research faculty; postdoctoral positions; Research Scientist, Senior/Principal Research Scientist; Methodologist; and other relevant academic or research roles.

Archive

Closed, expired, or removed opportunities are preserved in a separate searchable Archive rather than permanently deleted. Archived records retain their original First Shared date and include their last verification status.

Update Schedule

Deep searches are scheduled for Tuesday and Friday at 9:00 AM Eastern Time. “First Shared” is the date a position was first added to this board, not necessarily the employer’s posting date.

Sources

Sources are prioritized for direct, no-login access. Sources include HERC Jobs, HigherEd360, Inside Higher Ed Careers, Chronicle Jobs, HigherEdJobs, AERA Career Center, Psychometric Society Careers, and LinkedIn Jobs, along with relevant disciplinary and professional job boards. Official university and employer career pages are also searched and are preferred as the final source when an official posting is available.
