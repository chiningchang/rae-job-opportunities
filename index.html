About This Job Board

Purpose

This platform curates academic, postdoctoral, and research opportunities across the broad spectrum of educational research methodology—including quantitative methods, measurement, evaluation, educational data science, and AI-supported research design. It supports students, alumni, and colleagues exploring faculty, postdoctoral, research scientist, and other methodology-focused positions, including those connected with VCU’s Research, Assessment, and Evaluation (RAE) program, Advanced Educational Statistics Certificate, and CENTROID Applied Machine Learning Lab.

Search Process

The search process uses AI-assisted, multi-pass searches across academic job boards, disciplinary sources, and official employer career pages. Results are screened for methodological relevance, duplicates, and posting status. This curated collection should not be interpreted as exhaustive.

Search Areas

Opportunities are organized into seven broad areas: Measurement, Psychometrics, and Assessment; Statistical Theory and Quantitative Methodologies; Qualitative Methodologies; Multiple and Mixed Methodologies; Evaluation; Critical / Equity; and AI in Education. Selecting an area reveals frequently appearing subtopics identified from each posting’s Relevance terms, such as quantitative and qualitative research design, Bayesian methods, simulation, missing data, causal inference, SEM, multilevel / HLM, longitudinal methods, and learning analytics. A posting may match more than one area or subtopic.

Position Types

Assistant, Associate, Full, and Open Rank faculty; research faculty; postdoctoral positions; Research Scientist, Senior/Principal Research Scientist; Methodologist; and other relevant academic or research roles.

Archive

Opportunities that are closed, expired, removed, or no longer verifiable are preserved in a separate searchable Archive rather than permanently deleted. Archived records retain their original First Shared date and include their last verification status.

Update Schedule

Deep searches are scheduled for Tuesday and Friday at 2:00 AM Eastern Time. “First Shared” is the date a position was first added to this board, not necessarily the employer’s posting date.

Job Market Insights

The Job Market Insights page shows locations, fields, roles, stated application materials, and pay information for current active postings, alongside academic-year summaries that include active and archived postings. Pay and application-material information may be unavailable or unreported for some postings; applicants should confirm details with the hiring institution.

Sources

Opportunities are drawn from publicly available career listings and employer pages. When available, links lead to the hiring institution’s original posting.
