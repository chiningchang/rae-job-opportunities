# RAE Job Opportunities

A curated, regularly updated job board for U.S.-based academic and research opportunities aligned with **Virginia Commonwealth University’s Research, Assessment, and Evaluation (RAE) program**, **Advanced Educational Statistics Certificate**, and **CENTROID Applied Machine Learning Lab**.

**Live job board:** https://chiningchang.github.io/rae-job-opportunities/

## Purpose

This resource supports students, alumni, and colleagues seeking faculty, postdoctoral, research scientist, methodologist, and other research-focused positions related to educational research methodology.

The board emphasizes opportunities in:

- Quantitative methods and educational statistics
- Educational measurement and psychometrics
- Program evaluation and assessment
- Multilevel and hierarchical linear modeling
- Structural equation modeling and latent variable methods
- Longitudinal methods
- Qualitative and mixed methods
- Critical and equity-oriented research methods
- Machine learning and AI in education
- Educational data science, learning analytics, and educational data mining
- Educational research methodology and research design

## Job Board Features

The public site includes:

- Search by position, institution, methodology, or location
- Rank filtering
- Sorting by date first shared, institution, or position
- Interactive methodology overview and filters
- Methodology relevance tags
- Summary counts for active, faculty, and postdoctoral/research opportunities
- **NEW** indicators for recently shared opportunities
- Direct **View Posting** links

## Search and Update Process

A deep search is scheduled **every Tuesday and Friday at 9:00 AM Eastern Time**. The workflow searches multiple academic job boards, disciplinary sources, professional associations, and official university/employer career pages.

Listings are screened for methodological relevance, duplication, posting status, and link accessibility.

### Link verification

A search result or cached snippet is **not** treated as proof that a posting is active. Before a listing is retained on the public board, the destination link should:

1. Open without requiring a login.
2. Lead to the specific job posting rather than a generic careers homepage.
3. Not return a 404, “Not Found,” or similar error.
4. Still show the relevant position.

When possible, links are prioritized in this order:

1. Official university/employer specific posting
2. Established academic or professional job board
3. Other reputable no-login job source

Aggregators may be used to discover opportunities, but the workflow attempts to follow their application links to the original employer posting. **Indeed is not used as the final View Posting link**, and LinkedIn is primarily used for discovery when access may require a login.

Existing links are rechecked during scheduled updates. If a reliable, accessible posting can no longer be found, the listing is removed from the active board.

## Archive

Closed, expired, or removed opportunities are preserved in a separate **Archive** rather than permanently deleted. Archived records retain their original **First Shared** date and include **Last Verified** and **Status** information when verifiable. Archived jobs do not count toward active opportunity totals or receive NEW indicators.

## Data Notes

**First Shared** records the date a position was first added to this job board. It is not necessarily the date the employer originally posted the position.

Deadlines are reported only when they can be verified. If a deadline cannot be verified, it is listed as **Not listed**. **Open until filled** is used only when the posting explicitly indicates that status.
