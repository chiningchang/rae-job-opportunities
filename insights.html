About these data and how to read the charts

These figures describe a curated collection of U.S. academic and research positions, not the entire job market. The current map, role, method, application-material, and pay charts use active postings and respond to the available filters. The academic-year collection groups each unique posting by when it entered this collection (July 1 through June 30) and includes active and archived postings. Method areas can overlap, so their counts should not be added together. The seven areas are Measurement, Psychometrics, and Assessment; Statistical Theory and Quantitative Methodologies; Qualitative Methodologies; Multiple and Mixed Methodologies; Evaluation; Critical / Equity; and AI in Education. Area and subcategory counts are based on keywords in the board’s relevance summaries; an AI/ML mention does not necessarily indicate a role’s primary focus. Application-material counts include document requirements stated in postings, group equivalent names, and follow the role, method, and location filters. A posting that does not mention a document is not counted as requiring none. Pay information may be incomplete or unavailable, and figures follow the original posting. Annual observations draw on available descriptions and do not establish market growth or the wider prevalence of any role or method. Map locations are approximate; unknown locations remain in the posting list. Verify requirements and availability with each employer. Browse the full board and Archive.
