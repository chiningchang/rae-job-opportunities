About these data and how to read the charts

First Shared is the date a posting was added to this board, not the date the employer published it. This is a curated collection relevant to research, assessment, and evaluation; its counts do not measure the entire academic job market. The collection groups unique postings by academic year (July 1 through June 30) using the date each job was first shared here, and includes both active and archived records. Areas may overlap, so their counts should not be added together. Counts classify the board's short relevance summaries; the brief interpretations draw on available descriptions, which are incomplete for some archived jobs. This collection does not establish market growth or a comparison with prior years. A mention of AI/ML does not mean it is the primary focus of a role. Verify requirements and availability with each employer. Map positions are approximate and unknown locations remain in the posting list. Browse the full board and Archive.
