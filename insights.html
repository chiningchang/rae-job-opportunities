About these data and how to read the charts

These figures describe a curated collection of U.S. academic and research positions, not the entire job market. The current map, role, method, application-material, pay, and visa-sponsorship charts use active postings and respond to the available filters. The academic-year collection groups each unique posting by when it entered this collection (July 1 through June 30) and includes active and archived postings. Its expanded state, application-material, pay, and visa charts use that full selected-year collection and do not follow the current-opportunity filters; state colors show collected counts, not statewide job-market rates. Method areas can overlap, so their counts should not be added together. The seven areas are Measurement, Psychometrics, and Assessment; Statistical Theory and Quantitative Methodologies; Qualitative Methodologies; Multiple and Mixed Methodologies; Evaluation; Critical / Equity; and AI in Education. Area and subcategory counts are based on keywords in the board’s relevance summaries; an AI/ML mention does not necessarily indicate a role’s primary focus. Application-material counts include document requirements stated in postings, group equivalent names, and follow the role, method, and location filters. A posting that does not mention a document is not counted as requiring none. Pay information may be incomplete or unavailable, and figures follow the original posting. Visa sponsorship categories reflect what each available posting says: available, conditional or limited, not offered, not stated, or unable to assess. General visa sponsorship is not necessarily H-1B sponsorship; silence is not evidence of ineligibility. Policies may change, so applicants should confirm directly with the employer. Annual observations draw on available descriptions and do not establish market growth or the wider prevalence of any role or method. Appointment and institutional context follows the current filters or the selected academic-year collection, respectively. Appointment tracks require explicit evidence; rank alone does not establish tenure. Public/private control and Carnegie 2025 research designations refer to the verified institution or campus, not its department. RCU and no research designation are retained separately and do not imply a teaching-oriented institution. Teaching-load figures preserve their original units and periods; unlisted loads are not zero, and initial reductions are not the regular load. Context review dates are separate from the board update date. Map locations are approximate; unknown locations remain in the posting list. Verify requirements and availability with each employer.
