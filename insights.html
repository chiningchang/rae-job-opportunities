About the Data

Coverage, classification, and limitations.

Read the report methodology

This report describes a curated collection of educational research methodology job advertisements, not a census of all U.S. academic openings. Each posting is assigned to the academic year in which it entered the collection (July 1–June 30). Active and archived records are included; method themes may overlap. Compensation, teaching load, and visa language are summarized only when the relevant information is available and verified. Missing disclosure must not be interpreted as a negative response.

About the current dashboard and annual report

Current Market Snapshot displays currently active postings. Its methodology, position, location, application-material, pay, visa, and institutional-context charts respond to the active-opportunity filters. Annual Market Report is an academic-year report (July 1–June 30) using unique active and archived postings added to this curated collection; the dashboard filters do not affect the report. Monthly trends show the date postings entered the collection, not the employers' original advertisement dates. The annual report separates appointment tracks (Position Types), institution types and Carnegie 2025 research designations (Institutional Landscape), and reported teaching loads (Teaching Load). Appointment tracks require explicit evidence; academic rank alone does not establish tenure. Teaching-load reporting preserves original units and periods; a load that is not stated is not zero, and temporary reductions are not regular loads. Methodology themes may overlap, and keyword-based classifications do not necessarily indicate a position's primary focus. Salary, application-material and visa analyses reflect information available in reviewed postings; missing statements should not be interpreted as negative findings. Visa sponsorship language does not necessarily establish H-1B eligibility. Institution and research designations refer to the verified campus, not a department; RCU and no research designation are distinct. State maps show collected postings, not statewide employment rates. The data are curated rather than a comprehensive census, and annual counts do not establish national market growth. Verify details with each employer. See 10 · About the Data within the annual report for its scope and limitations, or browse the full board and archive.
