About these data and how to read the charts

First Shared is the date a posting was added to this board, not the date the employer published it. This is a curated collection relevant to research, assessment, and evaluation; its counts do not measure the entire academic job market. Research areas may overlap, and a mention of AI/ML does not mean it is the primary focus of a role. Verify requirements and availability with each employer.
