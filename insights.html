What materials might I need?

Common materials to prepare for academic applications.

  • Academic CVYour education, research, teaching, publications, and service.
  • Cover letterWhy this position fits your work and what you would contribute.
  • Research statementYour research agenda, accomplishments, and future plans.
  • Teaching statementYour approach to teaching, with concrete examples.
  • ReferencesNames and contact details, or letters when requested.

Some positions also request a diversity statement, writing sample, or sample syllabus. Requirements vary; check each posting.

About these data and how to read the charts

First Shared is the date a posting was added to this board, not the date the employer published it. This is a curated collection relevant to research, assessment, and evaluation; its counts do not measure the entire academic job market. Research areas may overlap, and a mention of AI/ML does not mean it is the primary focus of a role. Verify requirements and availability with each employer. Map positions are approximate and unknown locations remain in the posting list. Browse the full board and Archive.
